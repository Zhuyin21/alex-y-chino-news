# Personajes: who they are off the air

The characters' lives, so their anecdotes, opinions and jokes come from one consistent life. Built from Chuy's interview on Nov 19, 2026; it grows over time. **Read this before writing an episode.** PROGRAMACION.md has their roles on the show and the running bits; SHOW_BIBLE.md has how they talk. When this file and an older line elsewhere disagree, **this file wins**.

## How to use it on air
- **Anecdotes are short and casual:** one or two lines that come out of the conversation ("eso me pasó el martes que iba por la Coors…"), never a monologue, and at most one or two per episode between all of them.
- **Never contradict what's here or what already aired.** Before an anecdote, search recent scripts' Editor's logs for `Canon:` lines (`grep -h "Canon:" episodes/*.md`).
- **Every new personal detail or anecdote that airs gets a line in that episode's Editor's log:** `Canon: Chino — se le cayó el celular en la acequia (Ep 12)`. Chuy folds them into this file now and then.
- **Real places, no false claims about them.** Anecdotes can happen at the Big I, on Central, at the Balloon Fiesta or at a real restaurant, but nobody invents something bad about a real business or person (no "me enfermé en tal lugar"). Speaking well of a real place is fine; it's never a paid mention unless it really is one, and then it's announced as an ad.
- Details marked *(propuesta)* are Claude's suggestions, waiting for Chuy's OK: use them lightly until he confirms.

## Dr. Alex, "Doc"
| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | **Mid-forties.** "Ya es más don." |
| **From** | Burque, born and raised. Nuevomexicano de raíz: green chile, Northern New Mexico family traditions, the mix of Mexico and New Mexico. |
| **Lives** | **Northeast Heights**, with his wife and his daughter Julie. |
| **Drives** | A **Toyota RAV4**: reliable, practical, an engineer's car. |
| **Studies and work** | PhD in mechanical engineering. **Worked as an engineer at Intel in Rio Rancho** before the show. |
| **How he met Chino** | Chino interviewed him at the radio station where Chino used to work. Doc speaks Spanish, so they did the interview in Spanish, hit it off and became friends; the show came out of that friendship. |
| **How he met Ashley** | At a state science and technology fair where he was speaking. She asked great questions; she reminded him of Julie, they stayed in touch, and when the science segment opened he invited her. |

**Family**
- **His wife:** only mentioned in passing ("mi esposa dice que…"), no name or details on air.
- **Julie (12–13):** he pushes her toward science and engineering. Rare, short, proud-dad lines only; she's a minor, so no details about her school, looks or daily life.
- **His mamá:** an older, traditional lady from Burque who sends him videos on Facebook ("mi amá me mandó un video…").

**Personality and habits**
- Calm, clear, dry humor, laughs more than he lets on. Reserved and skeptical about AI.
- **His mania: he's always checking the car**: leaks, antifreeze, oil, tire pressure. Very dad, very engineer.
- Speaks New Mexican Spanish and English; trips on a hard Spanish word now and then.

**Tastes**
- **Chile: Christmas.** Very New Mexican, and that way he doesn't have to pick a side.
- **Food:** classic Burque spots, like **Frontier** across from UNM; '80s Burque comfort food.
- **Music:** old-school norteño and New Mexico music. **Sports:** Broncos. **Phone:** Samsung.

**Anecdote bank** *(propuestas, for Chuy's OK)*
1. The check-engine light that came on in the Intel parking lot: he diagnosed it himself with a code reader before anyone could call a tow truck. It was a loose gas cap.
2. Balloon Fiesta as a kid with his dad: up at 4 a.m., a breakfast burrito wrapped in foil, his hands frozen.
3. Julie's science fair volcano, which he "helped" with until it was more engineering project than volcano. His wife made him tone it down.
4. His mamá's video that he forwarded to the whole family before checking it was fake. He's never lived it down.

## Chino
| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | **25–27.** |
| **From** | **Juárez, Chihuahua.** His **abuelos** still live there. |
| **Lives** | **South Valley**, with his **mamá, papá and hermana** (mentioned only in passing). His tía and prima also live in Burque. |
| **Drives** | An **old Silverado** *(propuesta: around 2008)* with a **good sound system** for the corridos tumbados. He loves to go out **troquear** (cruising) on weekends. |
| **Studies and work** | Studied **Communication at UNM** and has worked a few years **in radio**. That's where he met Doc. |

**Family**
- Mamá, papá and hermana at home in the South Valley; the tía (Facebook queen) and the prima in Burque; the abuela in Juárez with her sayings; the **abuelo**, a **Raiders** fan since he worked in El Paso as a young man.

**Personality and habits**
- **Always running late,** always in a rush: that's why he trips, drops things and bursts into the studio out of breath.
- **Looks for the funny side of everything,** gets surprised by everything, and sometimes gets lost in the conversation ("…¿perdón, de qué hablábamos?").
- **Breaks or loses phones constantly** (dropped, cracked, in the water), which is why he's always on a different weird Android and always hunting deals on **Facebook Marketplace** ("¡está en oferta!"). A huge AI fan, sometimes too much.

**Tastes**
- **Food:** **food trucks**: Mexican food in the South Valley and along Central and San Mateo; the closer to Juárez, the better.
- **Chile:** he's a **salsa taquera** guy and brags that New Mexico green chile isn't that hot. *(Saved for later: one day he gets enchilado on air and Doc says "¿no que no picaba?". Could even tie into a sponsor.)*
- **Music:** corridos tumbados, reggaetón and Bad Bunny, new rap, and cumbia to dance. **Games:** **Warzone/Call of Duty, Valorant, GTA**. **Sports:** Cowboys and Chivas. **Phone:** whatever Android survived this month.

**Anecdote bank** *(propuestas, for Chuy's OK)*
1. His first week in Burque: he took the wrong exit at the Big I three times trying to get to UNM.
2. The phone that fell into the acequia while he was taking a video of his troca.
3. The "casi nuevo" subwoofer from Marketplace that only played the bass on one side.
4. Late to his own UNM graduation: they called his name while he was still parking.
5. Watching Raiders games with his abuelo in Juárez, who yelled at the TV in Spanish and English.
